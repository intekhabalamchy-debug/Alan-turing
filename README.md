# 💾 Alan Turing (1912–1954) — Pioneer of Modern Computing

> *"Sometimes it is the people no one can imagine anything of who do the things that no one can imagine."*  
> — **Alan Turing**

A digital memorial tribute honoring **Alan Mathison Turing OBE FRS** — mathematician, logician, Bletchley Park cryptanalyst, and founding father of theoretical computer science and artificial intelligence.

---

## 🏛️ Highlights & Features

- **🎖️ Archival Tribute Hero:** Split-screen hero featuring an archival portrait of Turing, biographical details, and his famous quote.
- **⚡ Key Breakthroughs Panorama (4 Milestones):**
  - **1936 — Universal Turing Machine:** Foundational architecture for algorithmic computation and all modern software.
  - **1939–1945 — Cracking the Enigma:** Hut 8 leadership and the electromechanical *Bombe* machine at Bletchley Park.
  - **1950 — The Turing Test:** The seminal "Computing Machinery and Intelligence" paper and Imitation Game.
  - **1952 — Mathematical Biology:** Morphogenesis and reaction-diffusion mathematical theory.
- **📜 Biographical Record & Chronology:** 8-point structured record covering early life, Cambridge & Princeton doctorates, WWII service, unjust conviction, and passing.
- **👑 Historic Recognition & Royal Pardon:** Coverage of Gordon Brown's 2009 state apology, Queen Elizabeth II's 2013 Royal Pardon, and the UK £50 banknote honour.
- **📱 Responsive Design:** Handcrafted layout built with semantic HTML5 and modern CSS Grid/Flexbox.
